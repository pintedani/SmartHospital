SmartHospital

Digital Patient Feedback Platform for Cluj County


Digitalization & Standardization of Patient Feedback

The Problem

📋 Paper-based questionnaires still used across hospitals — end up in plastic collection boxes, rarely digitized
🔀 Fragmented systems — each hospital uses its own method: QR posters, paper forms, or nothing at all
👴 Accessibility gap — elderly patients and children cannot easily provide feedback digitally
📊 No centralized analytics — hospital management lacks real-time insight into patient satisfaction trends

The Challenge

"Create a unified, digital, accessible, and county-wide standardized platform that replaces the bureaucracy of physical forms and centralizes patient feedback in a completely anonymous, transparent, and user-friendly way for all age groups."


1. Inclusive & Omnichannel Interface

2. Questionnaire Standardization (gamified, quick ratings)

3. Analytics Dashboard for Hospital Management

4. Abuse Reporting

Our Solution

✅ Unified platform — single system for all 10 hospitals in Cluj County
✅ Omnichannel feedback — Standard digital form, Audio-assisted input, and AI-powered document scanning
✅ AI-powered analytics — automatic interpretation, trend detection, and severity classification

🤖 AI-Powered Reports

Technology Stack

Frontend

React + TypeScript, Material UI, Vite, i18n (RO/EN)

Backend

.NET 8 Web API, Entity Framework Core, SQLite, SignalR (real-time alerts)

AI / ML

LLM integration (Anthropic-compatible), Tesseract OCR for document scanning

Infrastructure

Identity authentication, role-based access, anonymous feedback with tracking codes
